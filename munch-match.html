What I did

A healthy eating app that helps college students track nutrition and find recipes that fit their goals, grounded in interviews with students on campus.

  • Led a 5-person team designing a nutrition-tracking app for college students, grounded directly in campus user research interviews.
  • Led user interviews with 13 beginner cooks and synthesized the findings into five design insights that shaped the persona, storyboards, and feature priorities.
  • Drove the UX design from low-fidelity sketches and mid-fidelity wireframes to a high-fidelity Figma prototype, revising it through peer critique and usability evaluations.
  • Delivered a high-fidelity app and companion website showcasing key features and the design process through interactive, clickable Figma prototypes.

Design process

Read the case study ↗

How might we help busy young adults who are new to cooking build healthy, lasting eating habits, given their limited time, budget, and kitchen resources?

  1. Research

    Compared five existing healthy eating apps, and I led interviews with 13 beginner cooks aged 19 to 24. Five needs stood out: too little time, tight grocery budgets, apps that make cooking and nutrition tracking feel complicated, limited cooking skills, and cooking that feels like a chore.

  2. Persona and storyboards

    Turned the interview findings into a persona of a busy student who relies on takeout, and two storyboards: cooking challenges shared with friends, and quick, low-cost recipes for a tight schedule.

  3. Sketches and wireframes

    Explored recipe browsing, step-by-step recipe pages with video, a nutrition log, and weekly challenges in low-fidelity sketches, then refined the home, recipe, and nutrition analysis screens as mid-fidelity wireframes.

  4. High-fidelity prototype

    Built the full interface in Figma as a clickable prototype.

  5. Usability testing

    Revised the design after peer critique and mini-evaluations: larger body text, a single-column filter page with collapsible categories, higher-contrast buttons and navigation, a clearer servings control, a more consistent color palette, and a simpler main flow without the separate friends page.
