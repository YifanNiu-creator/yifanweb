Interview Notes

Notes from interviews with three students whose first Ithaca winter was a big change from home: Shanghai, Irvine, and New Delhi. Each student answered the same seven questions.

Shanghai, China

Interviewee 1

1Where are you from, and what were winters like there?

  • The interviewee is from Shanghai, a city in southeastern China.
  • Winter temperatures are generally about 10°C warmer than in Ithaca.
  • Temperatures rarely drop below freezing, and light snowfall only occurs once every few years.

2Before experiencing your first winter in Ithaca, what were your biggest concerns?

  • Initially underestimated how cold Ithaca could get.
  • Was not adequately prepared in terms of clothing or expectations.
  • Only brought two cashmere coats and one windproof down jacket.
  • Did not expect the cold weather to have such a significant impact on daily transportation and mobility.

3Before your first winter in Ithaca, what did you most want to know about preparing for it?

  • Initially underestimated how cold Ithaca could get, so did almost no preparing.

4How did you look for information or advice about preparing for your first winter in Ithaca? Which sources did you use, and why?

  • Mainly used social media to research winter clothing.
  • Looked at what clothing brands other international students on the East Coast were buying.
  • Also visited clothing brands' official websites to explore winter clothing options.

5What challenges did you face when preparing for your first winter in Ithaca?

  • Consulted the winter preparation guide provided by the Chinese Students and Scholars Association (CSSA).
  • Felt that some recommendations encouraged overpreparation.
  • Found it difficult to understand what local students actually wear during winter.
  • Wanted more realistic, everyday examples of winter outfits.

6What would make information about preparing for winter easier for you to find and understand?

  • Prefers information shared through social media.
  • Photos and videos would make winter preparation advice easier to understand.
  • Would appreciate seeing specific examples of what people actually purchased and wore.

7Looking back, what do you wish you had known before experiencing your first winter in Ithaca? What advice would you give to students who are new to Ithaca?

  • Frequently slipped on icy sidewalks and wished there had been more information about safe winter transportation and practical tools for navigating snow and ice.
  • Recommended sharing information about warm, welcoming indoor spaces where students can spend time during winter.
  • Recommended Durland Alternatives Library, a cozy place in the church across from Hollister Hall, next to the Law School. It has books, coffee, pillows, and sometimes small markets. It's a nice place to stay warm and relax during winter.
  • Suggested introducing similar places to help new students feel more comfortable during the winter months.

Irvine, California

Interviewee 2

1Where are you from, and what were winters like there?

  • The interviewee is from Irvine, California, where there is almost no winter.
  • Temperatures are usually around 11–12°C during winter.
  • Had never experienced snowfall before coming to Ithaca.

2Before experiencing your first winter in Ithaca, what were your biggest concerns?

  • Wanted to know what clothes to wear to stay warm, especially since indoor heating is strong while outdoor temperatures can drop to -20°C.
  • Was concerned about slipping on icy sidewalks and wanted to know what kinds of shoes to wear.
  • Wanted to understand how apartment heating works, whether it needs to stay on all the time, and how much it would cost.

3Before your first winter in Ithaca, what did you most want to know about preparing for it?

  • Same as the previous question.

4How did you look for information or advice about preparing for your first winter in Ithaca? Which sources did you use, and why?

  • Looked at social media posts about what Cornell students wear during winter.
  • Also searched Reddit for advice.
  • Mainly relied on advice from older students who had already experienced winters in Ithaca.

5What challenges did you face when preparing for your first winter in Ithaca?

  • Everyone has a different tolerance for cold, so it was hard to know which advice would actually work.
  • There were many winter-related problems they had never thought about before arriving, so they didn't even know what to search for.

6What would make information about preparing for winter easier for you to find and understand?

  • Photos and videos would make the information easier to understand.
  • Would prefer one comprehensive website that brings together different types of winter preparation advice.
  • Wants the information organized into clear categories so students can easily find what they need.

7Looking back, what do you wish you had known before experiencing your first winter in Ithaca? What advice would you give to students who are new to Ithaca?

  • Don't worry too much about the cold. It may not feel as bad as you expect.
  • Pay attention to winter activities at Cornell, such as skiing.
  • Make the most of the snow and enjoy the winter experience while you're here.

New Delhi, India

Interviewee 3

1Where are you from, and what were winters like there?

I'm from New Delhi, where winters are generally pretty mild! Temperatures don't usually drop below 60°F during the day, although they can dip to around 35°F at night. The biggest concern in Delhi during the winter isn't really the cold, but the air pollution, which can reach an AQI of 500 at times. The cold itself, however, isn't a significant concern.

2Before experiencing your first winter in Ithaca, what were your biggest concerns?

I was definitely worried about the cold. I knew temperatures in Ithaca could drop below 0°F, which was pretty intimidating because I'd never experienced that kind of cold for prolonged periods of time. Coming from a place where summer temperatures can reach 120°F, which is uncomfortable but something I'm used to, and where winters are relatively mild, I was concerned about how I'd adjust to such a drastic change in climate. I was especially worried about the lack of sunlight and how that might affect me.

3Before your first winter in Ithaca, what did you most want to know about preparing for it?

Definitely how to stay warm and, perhaps more importantly, how to stay happy during the gloomy days. I find that my mood can be pretty affected by a lack of sunlight, so one of my biggest priorities was figuring out how to prepare for that and finding ways to keep my spirits up during the winter.

Staying warm was obviously another concern, but I found that with a little research, it wasn't too difficult to find good jackets, boots, and other winter essentials.

4How did you look for information or advice about preparing for your first winter in Ithaca? Which sources did you use, and why?

I had family friends who had either attended Cornell or taught here, so I primarily turned to them for advice based on their personal experiences rather than relying solely on online resources. I found it helpful to hear directly from people who had actually experienced Ithaca winters and could offer practical recommendations.

5What challenges did you face when preparing for your first winter in Ithaca?

I think the biggest challenge was navigating the sheer number of options available, especially when it came to winter clothing. There are so many different jackets, boots, and brands to choose from, and everyone seems to have a different opinion about what's best. It was pretty overwhelming trying to figure out what I actually needed versus what was simply nice to have, and making choices that felt right for me was definitely challenging.

6What would make information about preparing for winter easier for you to find and understand?

I think having a consolidated resource list outlining what students actually need for an Ithaca winter, along with recommendations for where to find those items, would be incredibly helpful. Something like a winter preparation guide from Cornell itself, or even a resource sheet circulated through the dorms, could make the process much easier for incoming students.

It would be especially useful to have recommendations for reliable outerwear brands, different price points, and explanations of which items are essential versus optional.

7Looking back, what do you wish you had known before experiencing your first winter in Ithaca? What advice would you give to students who are new to Ithaca?

I wish I'd known that Ithaca winters aren't necessarily as miserable as they sound! The lack of sunlight can be challenging, but winter can also be really beautiful. Experiencing my first proper snowfall here was wonderful, especially watching it from the warmth of my room.

For practical advice, I'd definitely recommend investing in waterproof shoes, a good winter coat, and a sunrise alarm clock to make those dark mornings a little easier. I'd also say learn how to layer! It can be freezing outside and absolutely boiling indoors, so wearing a tank top under a sweater and jacket worked really well for me.

Finally, don't feel like you have to sacrifice your personal style! I have a gigantic Super Puff for the coldest days, but I also love wearing my wool coat when the weather allows. With the right preparation, Ithaca winters are actually pretty manageable, and there are lots of little things to enjoy about them!

Audience goals

What the three interviews added up to. These six goals guided the content and structure of the website.

  1. Prepare for cold weather

    Students want to understand how cold Ithaca winters actually feel and what clothing and shoes they need to stay warm and safe.

  2. Navigate safely during winter

    Students want to learn how to get around campus safely in snowy and icy conditions, including walking and using public transportation.

  3. Find reliable and practical information

    Students want realistic advice from people who have experienced Ithaca winters, rather than general recommendations that may lead to overpreparation.

  4. Understand winter living essentials

    Students want to know how to manage indoor heating, heating costs, and other everyday challenges during winter.

  5. Access information easily

    Students want a comprehensive website with clear categories, photos, and videos so they can quickly find and understand the information they need.

  6. Enjoy winter in Ithaca

    Students want to discover winter activities, cozy indoor spaces, and ways to make their first winter in Ithaca more enjoyable.
