What I did

Cornell Winter Survival Kit gathers practical winter advice in one place: what to wear, how to get around on snow and ice, how to stay healthy, and how to have fun, written for students who have never lived through a cold winter.

  • Defined the concept for Cornell Winter Survival Kit, a website that helps students new to cold climates prepare for, and enjoy, winter in Ithaca.
  • Analyzed two existing winter websites for theme, responsive behavior, and reusable design patterns.
  • Planned and conducted interviews with three students, then translated the notes into six audience goals that guided the content.
  • Organized the content through physical card sorting into four topics and a five-page navigation.
  • Sketched two homepage concepts and wide and narrow screen layouts for every page, using familiar patterns such as icon cards, a top navigation bar, and clearly separated sections.

Design process

How might a website help students who have never lived through a cold winter feel prepared for Ithaca, and even enjoy it, without feeling overwhelmed?
