Comparative Analysis

Two existing winter websites, studied for their theme, how they respond to screen size, and the design patterns worth reusing.

On this page

  1. Ready.gov: Winter Ready
  2. Winter CMS

Ready.gov: Winter Ready

ready.gov/winter-ready ↗
Theme and feeling
Focused on winter preparedness and safety. The clean, professional design creates a sense of reliability and reassurance, helping visitors feel more confident about preparing for winter.
Wide and narrow screens
On wide screens, content sits in a horizontal layout with clearly separated sections. On narrow screens, it adjusts into a vertical, stacked layout.
What I wanted to reuse
A large, winter-themed illustrated banner that communicates the purpose of the site at once, clear navigation, distinct background colors to separate sections, and one consistent color palette throughout.

Winter CMS

wintercms.com ↗
Theme and feeling
A cool blue palette creates a strong winter atmosphere. The clean, modern design feels calm, fresh, and simple.
Wide and narrow screens
Four content cards sit in a single row on wide screens and stack on narrow ones. Images and text sit side by side on wide screens and stack vertically on narrow screens.
What I wanted to reuse
The consistent blue palette, the large snowy mountain background, and rounded white cards that overlap the background image: a card layout with large icons, clear headings, and soft shadows.
